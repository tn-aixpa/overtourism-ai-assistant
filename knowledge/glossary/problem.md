# Problem

A critical situation or phenomenon to be analyzed or managed within the platform . A Problem is the top-level concept: it owns one or more Proposals.

## Examples

- Overcrowding in a specific location
- Lack of parking in a city during summer season
- Mobility congestion during peak season
