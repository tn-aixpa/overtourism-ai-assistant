# Uncertainty

A measure of the confidence associated with a prediction or simulation result for a Scenario. Uncertainty reflects the expected reliability of the model output and arises from (i) assumptions about people behaviour and other tourism-related processes, and (ii) approximations introduced by the statistical and computational models.
