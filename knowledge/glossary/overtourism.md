# Overtourism

We are in an overtourism situation when the demand on a destination exceeds one or more of its Constraint capacities, causing negative impacts on residents, infrastructure, or the environment. These situations can be mapped as Problems.

## Examples
- The number of cars reaching the city center is higher than the number of cars that can be parked in the nearby area.
