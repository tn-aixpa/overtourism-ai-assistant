# Territorial Granularity

Territorial granularity is the geographic level at which a territorial index is grouped or shown, not its time period. In this platform, it means choosing whether to view the index by municipality (comune), tourism macro-area (APT), or for the whole province (Provincia). Municipality and APT areas cannot be mixed in the same selection; the provincial value can be shown on its own or alongside selected areas.
