# Proposal

A possible strategy or intervention designed to address a specific Problem. A Proposal groups one or more Scenarios that explore different parameter configurations for that strategy. Each Proposal belongs to exactly one Problem.

## Examples

- Introduce visitor caps
- Promote off-peak mobility incentives
