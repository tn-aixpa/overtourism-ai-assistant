# Criticality index

A measure of how often one or more constraints exceeds their respective capacity limits during a simulation. It is expressed as the proportion of time or simulation outcomes in which the selected constraint (or all selected constraints) are exceeded. A higher criticality value indicates that capacity limits are violated more often.
